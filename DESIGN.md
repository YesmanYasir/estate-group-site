# Design system

## Creative concept
**The Mediterranean, drawn in light.** The site feels like an architectural maquette: deep mineral green, limestone planes, a fine copper survey mark, quiet coordinates, and generous property photography. The 3D scene is one authored signature, not a page-wide gimmick.

## Palette
- Pine `#14201D` — hero, footer, primary text
- Limestone `#E9E4D8` — warm structural surfaces
- Paper `#F7F5EF` — main canvas
- Oxidized copper `#B77D58` — restrained emphasis
- Sea-glass `#82968A` — scene material and secondary accent
- Stone `#817D73` — supporting text

## Type and layout
Playfair Display carries the editorial headlines; DM Sans carries labels and interface text. Use generous side gutters, hairline rules, asymmetrical but stable grids, sharp rectangular actions, and minimal surface decoration. Keep cards photo-led and avoid generic SaaS widgets.

## Responsive and accessible
Mobile uses a real nav drawer, one-column property cards, static-friendly scene fallback, visible focus, meaningful alt text, and native controls. Reduced-motion keeps all content and interactions available.
